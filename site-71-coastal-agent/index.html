About

Isabel Moreno

Isabel leads The Moreno Group from Newport Beach. She advises families who buy and sell along this shore — village streets in Corona del Mar, bluffs in Newport Coast, and the islands of the harbor.

The practice is private. Many introductions never reach the open market. Pricing is specific, and the coast is the only place she works.
