Casa Lumen  /  Paradise Valley

A house drawn
in light and stone.

Six bedrooms, a horizon pool and four acres of desert garden, offered privately for the first time since its completion in 2021.

Scroll

I   Arrival

The door opens
onto the light.

A pivoting walnut door, four metres tall, and a gallery of honed limestone that runs straight through to the view.

II   The Living Room

A room built
around the horizon.

Floor-to-ceiling glass on three sides, a travertine hearth and a ceiling that disappears into the dusk.

III   The Kitchen

Stone, oak
and quiet.

A single slab of Calacatta on the island, joinery in rift-sawn white oak, and a scullery hidden behind it.

IV   The Dining Room

A table for twelve,
a view for all.

Set against the glass so that every chair faces the valley as the light goes.

V   The Primary Suite

Wake to
the mountain.

A private wing with its own terrace, dressing rooms and the first light over Camelback.

VI   The Spa Bath

A bath carved
from one stone.

A sculpted stone tub before a single pane of glass, heated floors and a steam room beyond.

VII   The Water

The pool holds
the last hour of day.

Set flush with the terrace and lined in honed basalt, the horizon pool reads as a single dark plane.

VIII   The Land

Four acres,
one quiet line.

Native palo verde, ironwood and agave, arranged so that no neighbour is visible from any room.
