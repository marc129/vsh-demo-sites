Four views of the estate: interior, mooring, arrival, and airside.

Fifteen acres of headland and one house upon it, cliff on three sides, a beach on the fourth. The surface of the sea rises to the ridge, falls to a pool, and meets the sky.

Built from the cliff

The house is cut into the rock rather than set upon it. Walls take the colour of the headland. Glass is only where the view has earned it.

Seven rooms that matter, measured to the inch and placed to the view.

1 466Square metres

  1. 01The long room

    Fire, dining, and the western glass in one volume. The cliff is the fourth wall.

  2. 02The sleeping court

    A private court off the primary room, and the sound of water under the terrace.

  3. 03The pool

    Water cut into the headland. At half six it takes the colour of the sky.

  4. 04The cliff stair

    Landings, not flights, from the ridge down to the beach.

  5. 05The mooring

    A berth in the lee of the headland, reached on foot when the tide allows.

  6. 06The arrival court

    Gravel and shadow. The door is not on the sea side.

  7. 07The airside

    A pad on the ridge, for the days the road is not the point.
